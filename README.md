# HR Analytics Dashboard

A dashboard about the workforce of a company, built to look at employee attrition (people leaving). It covers 1,480 employee records.

## The problem
When employees leave, it costs time and money. I wanted to see who is leaving and from where, so HR could spot patterns in age, gender, job role, years at the company and salary band.

## What you can explore
- Headline numbers: employee count (1.48K), average age (36.9) and average years at the company (7.0)
- Attrition by age group, gender, education field, years at company and job role
- A salary slab filter and a job role filter that update every chart
- A detail table of individual records

## What I found
- The biggest age group is 26 to 35 (611 employees), followed by 36 to 45 (471). Numbers fall steadily after that.
- There are more men (889) than women (591) in the data.
- Laboratory Technician is the largest job role (261 employees), followed by Manufacturing Director (147) and Healthcare Representative (132).
- Most employees are still early in their time at the company. The years-at-company chart is highest in the first few years and drops sharply after that.
